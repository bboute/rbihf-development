## Why

“Development Hub” is generic and does not express the national role or high-performance ambition of RBIHF’s player, coach, and club development work. Renaming the digital platform to **RBIHF Development Centre of Excellence (RDCE)** gives it a distinctive, memorable identity.

## What Changes

- Replace uses of “Development Hub” as the platform’s name with **RBIHF Development Centre of Excellence** across user-facing application surfaces and relevant project documentation. The navigation may omit the prefix in the descriptor because it already displays “RBIHF” as the wordmark directly above.
- Use **RDCE** as the short form where a concise name is appropriate.
- Preserve unrelated meanings of “development hub” and historical references where changing them would misrepresent context.

## Capabilities

This is a branding-only change and does not alter product behavior or introduce a capability. Specs are intentionally skipped via `skip_specs: true`.

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Potentially affected surfaces include application metadata, navigation and page copy, footer branding, and project documentation. No APIs, dependencies, or user workflows are intended to change.
